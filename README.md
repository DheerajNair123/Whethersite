# CityTemp: Instant Temperature Insights

Welcome to **CityTemp**, your quick and reliable source for current temperature data from cities around the world. Our mission is to provide you with instant, straightforward temperature information—no distractions, just the essentials.

## Features:

- **City Temperature Lookup**: Easily find the current temperature for cities worldwide with a simple search. We provide only the temperature—nothing else to clutter your view.
- **Quick Access**: Enjoy fast and responsive updates, ensuring you get the temperature you need in real time, with no delays or unnecessary details.
- **Global Coverage**: Access temperature data for major cities across all continents. Whether you're in New York or Nairobi, CityTemp has you covered.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Designed with simplicity in mind, CityTemp's clean layout allows you to navigate quickly and easily to the temperature information you're looking for.

At **CityTemp**, we believe in keeping things simple. Whether you're planning your next trip, figuring out what to wear, or just curious about the weather in your favorite cities, CityTemp delivers the temperature insights you need—fast.
